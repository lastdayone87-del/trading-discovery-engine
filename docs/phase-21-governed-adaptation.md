# Phase 21 — Governed corrective learning and safe adaptation

Phase 21 completes the remaining dependency-ordered roadmap contracts without
turning the engine into an unconstrained self-modifying system.

## Corrective learning

An authoritative human approval of a previously non-terminal channel schedules
an observational diagnosis only after the review transaction commits. Diagnosis
uses the prior immutable classification diagnostic and distinguishes retrieval,
provider, evidence, semantic, corroboration, calibration, and policy gaps. The
result is an immutable incident plus a source-bound remediation proposal.

No incident changes a query, concept, provider policy, calibration, or serving
decision. Proposals must enter the existing query, knowledge, evaluation, or
policy governance path. The observer is default-off and cannot fail review.

## Review allocation

Review capacity has three explicit lanes: protected random audit, operational
adjudication, and active learning. Protected audits are allocated first and are
never displaced by uncertainty sampling. Active-learning utility combines model
disagreement, calibration uncertainty, slice scarcity, and concept novelty while
deduplicating channels and limiting one selected member per correlated cluster.
Every allocation records its lane, selection reason, and inclusion propensity.

## Coverage, saturation, and drift

Coverage gaps and saturation are one portfolio signal rather than competing
subsystems. Marginal unique yield, confirmation yield, uncertainty, cost, and
time since probe determine opportunity. Low-yield cells retain scheduled probes;
small samples explicitly abstain from saturation.

Drift detection also abstains below minimum sample sizes. Its only outputs are
bounded proposals such as an audit or reactivation. It cannot update a model,
catalog, weight, or allocation directly.

## Experiments, operational attribution, and provider calibration

The common experiment ledger supports registered action families, bounded
allocation, the shared utility contract, immutable assignment/outcome events,
guardrail stops, and explicit promotion proposals. Operational diagnoses retain
change manifests, confidence, counterevidence, and bounded recommendations.

Provider reliability is represented only by immutable, segmented, out-of-sample
calibration intervals. Sparse segments remain `SHADOW`; there are no continuously
mutable per-request provider weights and no provider self-rating.

All controls install disabled, automatic publication remains false, and none of
these artifacts has serving or automatic-promotion authority.
