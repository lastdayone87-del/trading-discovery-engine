# Phase 20 — Temporal, entity-resolved research frontier

## Purpose

This phase expands discovery beyond query-visible creators without weakening
classification. It builds on Phase 19 identities and source families rather than
creating a second identity system. Creator, channel, firm, broker, exchange,
platform, website, community, and concept relationships are observations—not
facts silently overwritten in a mutable graph.

## Temporal and provenance contract

Every relationship observation pins both endpoints, relationship type, source
family, optional immutable artifact, source locator, confidence, extractor
version, observation time, and validity interval. Corrections are append-only
retractions. An as-of projection selects the latest observation known at that
time, so historical investigations remain reproducible and future evidence
cannot leak into an earlier replay.

Sealed frontier snapshots pin their observation keys, policy version, entity
resolution policy, cutoff, definition, and checksum. Runs pin a snapshot, seed
entities, policy, mode, selection propensity, and actor. Candidate paths retain
every parent entity and their depth rather than exposing an unexplained graph
score.

## Precision and abuse boundaries

The planner uses only resolved entity identifiers. It filters low-confidence and
stale relationships, collapses support by source family, and applies hard depth,
fan-out, generic-hub, and component caps. Cross-source policies can require more
than one independent family. A capped, stale, or insufficiently independent
candidate remains visible with its reason but is not traversed.

The mutable graph has no serving authority. A frontier candidate is only a
research lead: it cannot confirm a creator, lower staged-classification gates,
publish knowledge, or automatically enqueue unbounded acquisition. Controls are
installed paused, killed, quota-free, and in `SHADOW`; canary mode remains bounded
and separately governed.

## Measurement and promotion

Outcomes distinguish incremental confirmed creators from already-known creators,
review, rejection, and unreachable entities. They record provider cost, latency,
evidence, and an optional retrieval-bound evaluation assignment. Promotion must
compare equal-budget frontier and query controls using incremental confirmed
creators unavailable to the contemporaneous control, terminal precision,
confirmation recall, review rate, provider units per confirmation, time to
confirmation, false-link corrections, language/script slices, and hub/component
cap rates.

No aggregate discovery lift may compensate for a terminal-precision regression.
The evaluation control plane remains the promotion authority; a frontier run or
outcome cannot activate production. Rollback pauses and kills the control while
preserving immutable observations, snapshots, runs, candidates, and outcomes for
audit and replay.
