# Principal architecture review — post-roadmap evolution

**Status:** Architectural assessment, not an implementation commitment  
**Review date:** 2026-08-01  
**Scope:** The assembled production and governed-shadow architecture after Priorities 0, 1, 2, 7, 8, and 10 and the recent production integration work

## Executive judgment

The engine is **close to the right target architecture at the level of boundaries**. Open-world abstention, staged decisions, field provenance, immutable observations, governed publications, controlled trials, durable search work, and last-known-good fallbacks are the difficult foundational choices; replacing them would create risk rather than progress.

The system is not yet close to its attainable operating performance. Its remaining constraint is no longer a missing ontology or another classifier feature. The constraint is that several sound components still form a mostly fixed pipeline:

- evidence is acquired in a predetermined order rather than selected for the uncertainty of the individual case;
- correctness is measured primarily on labels that happen to arrive, rather than on an intentionally sampled and statistically usable evaluation population;
- the evidence graph and portfolio policy are governed foundations, but not yet a production research controller with diverse executable actions;
- durable jobs exist, but a classification/enrichment attempt is not yet a fully idempotent, resumable workflow;
- adaptation reacts to accumulated yield more readily than to detected changes in language, source, or coverage distributions.

Therefore this review recommends **three high-impact investments and two medium-impact investments**. They are deliberately orthogonal to static keyword growth and do not add country-specific rules. The first two should precede broader model or adapter expansion: without them, the engine cannot reliably know which expansion improves recall or how to spend evidence quota on it.

## Prioritization criteria

Recommendations are ranked by their ability to change the engine's attainable precision/recall frontier, not by implementation novelty. The review favors work that:

1. improves decisions across languages and countries rather than for one market;
2. creates information the engine cannot obtain today;
3. preserves affirmative terminal claims and safe abstention;
4. has a measurable, reversible production rollout;
5. compounds the value of the existing governed knowledge plane instead of duplicating it.

## High-impact / worth implementing

### 1. Build a decision-grade evaluation and calibration control plane

**Recommendation: implement now. This is the highest priority.**

#### Limitation today

The repository has immutable classification diagnostics, shadow comparisons, review labels, replay, and offline evaluation. Those are necessary instrumentation, but the label population is still generated by product behavior: uncertain cases are more likely to reach review, confirmed and terminal-negative cases are less likely to be checked, and discoveries are conditioned on the current query policy. Shadow metrics currently reduce this population to aggregate accuracy, precision, recall, and disagreement counts. The multilingual semantic bootstrap is explicitly conservative rather than a representative fitted calibration.

Consequently, a higher measured recall can reflect a different review mix, and apparently stable precision can hide a language, script, sparse-evidence, or provider-availability regression. The engine can replay what it saw, but cannot yet estimate reliably what it missed.

#### Why this is architectural

This is a data-generation and causal-measurement boundary, not a missing dashboard metric. Once the serving policy decides which creators are retrieved, enriched, and reviewed, naive labels are selection-biased. No classifier implementation can repair that bias after the fact. Calibration artifacts, promotion gates, query rewards, and knowledge publication all depend on a common definition of ground truth and sampling provenance.

#### Proposed change

Create a versioned evaluation control plane shared by classification, acquisition, and discovery:

1. Define immutable evaluation cohorts at the **retrieval boundary**, before country and trading decisions remove candidates.
2. Assign a small, budgeted stratified audit sample across terminal positives, terminal negatives, uncertain cases, country rejections, sparse/rich evidence, provider states, discovery origins, languages, scripts, and time windows.
3. Persist inclusion probability and assignment policy so inverse-propensity or doubly robust estimates are possible. Do not infer propensities retrospectively.
4. Use blinded double review and adjudication on a smaller calibration subset. Represent label disagreement and hierarchical truth (trading relevance, creator type, country/audience, community existence) rather than forcing every ambiguity into one class.
5. Produce immutable time-split calibration artifacts and confidence intervals by supported slice. Require minimum effective sample size; otherwise report `INSUFFICIENT_EVALUATION_COVERAGE` rather than a misleading metric.
6. Join delayed outcomes, such as later community confirmation or review correction, back to the original decision without rewriting it.
7. Make promotion gates consume these artifacts for classifier policies, semantic providers, knowledge publications, and query portfolios.

This is not a request for universal manual labeling. Sampling rates should be optimized for information gain, with a protected random baseline that adaptive sampling cannot eliminate.

#### Expected impact

- **Classifier quality and precision:** makes threshold and calibration changes defensible, especially where false positives are rare.
- **Confirmation recall:** exposes false negatives in terminal and pre-classification gates rather than measuring only the review queue.
- **Multilingual capability:** identifies whether weakness is language semantics, retrieval coverage, evidence availability, or calibration.
- **Governance and explainability:** every promotion can state the population, policy, time range, confidence interval, and review provenance supporting it.
- **Scalability:** prevents review volume from growing linearly with traffic by allocating labels where they reduce uncertainty most.

#### Trade-offs and cost

- Sustained human-review cost is unavoidable; the system should reserve a fixed review budget rather than treat review as overflow.
- Rare-language confidence intervals will initially be wide. That is an honest limitation, not a reason to pool incompatible slices silently.
- Sampling terminal negatives carries operational sensitivity. Audit results must remain non-serving until adjudicated, and rescans must use a separately authorized lane.
- Data retention and reviewer access need explicit privacy controls because raw field snapshots may contain external-page or transcript material.
- Initial complexity is medium-high, but provider quota impact is low unless audit samples deliberately request additional evidence.

#### Exit gate

Do not promote a new authoritative classifier or materially alter query reward until the control plane can report time-split precision, recall, abstention, and review cost with confidence intervals for the major evidence and language slices.

### 2. Replace fixed enrichment with a budgeted value-of-information evidence controller

**Recommendation: implement now, after the evaluation cohort contract is fixed.**

#### Limitation today

The unified ingestion pipeline makes a fixed sequence of decisions. An uncertain first pass schedules one enrichment pass; an uncertain enrichment pass becomes review. Country hydration is a special pre-classification call, and governed evidence is evaluated after the production classifier under a rollout flag. This is safe, but it treats materially different uncertainty states alike.

A creator missing recent descriptions, a code-switched creator needing transcript evidence, a creator with an unresolved external community link, and a creator with strong contradictory evidence do not have the same best next action. A generic extra pass can spend quota without changing the decision, while high-value evidence may remain unavailable before the trading gate.

#### Why this is architectural

Classification and acquisition are currently separated as if evidence were a static input. In an open-world system, deciding **which observation to obtain next** is part of inference. Adding another provider to the fixed chain only increases cost and latency; it does not solve action selection, stopping, or attribution.

#### Proposed change

Introduce a durable `EvidenceCase` and `EvidenceAction` protocol:

- Providers and adapters declare prerequisites, cost distribution, latency service level, freshness, applicable field/language support, and the kind of uncertainty they may resolve.
- Each classification stage emits explicit unresolved hypotheses, such as `COUNTRY_EVIDENCE_MISSING`, `SEMANTIC_LABEL_AMBIGUOUS`, `CORROBORATION_DOCUMENT_REQUIRED`, or `COMMUNITY_ENTITY_UNRESOLVED`.
- A deterministic baseline policy maps those hypotheses to eligible actions and estimates expected decision value from the evaluation control plane: probability of changing a useful decision, confidence improvement, review avoidance, and discovery value minus quota, latency, and failure cost.
- The controller selects one bounded action at a time, persists the assignment and propensity, reclassifies against the accumulated immutable evidence bundle, and stops on a terminal decision, budget ceiling, deadline, dominance of contradiction, or low expected value.
- High-cost semantic, transcript, external-page, playlist, or community inspection becomes conditional rather than universal. Provider failure remains an operational observation and never semantic evidence.
- The existing fixed enrichment path remains the fallback policy and can be compared in randomized shadow/canary cohorts.

The controller must choose only from registered, governed actions. It is not an LLM agent and must not invent tools or relax terminal thresholds.

#### Expected impact

- **Confirmation recall:** obtains the missing corroborating source most likely to resolve a genuine creator.
- **Precision:** preserves staged terminal gates and avoids interpreting failed acquisition as negative evidence.
- **Quota and latency:** reduces low-value calls; permits higher spend only for cases with measurable expected value.
- **Multilingual capability:** selects transcript, transliteration, or semantic adjudication based on observed fields instead of country defaults.
- **Explainability:** adds a causal trace: what was unknown, why an action was chosen, what it returned, and why the controller stopped.
- **Autonomous adaptation:** learns action effectiveness by evidence state without allowing a model to modify policy directly.

#### Trade-offs and cost

- High implementation complexity: workflow state, action idempotency, counterfactual evaluation, deadlines, and concurrency control must agree.
- Per-candidate latency may increase because acquisition becomes sequential. Bound wall-clock time and allow parallel execution only for actions whose value exceeds their combined cost.
- Early value estimates will be weak. Begin with a deterministic cost table and shadow assignments; learn estimates only from randomized exploration with recorded propensities.
- Transcript and external-page actions can have material quota, egress, legal, and retention costs. They should be optional capabilities with explicit policy scope.
- Review load may rise initially because the controller will expose cases previously lost as negatives; the review budget must be a first-class constraint.

#### Exit gate

Canary only when the controller demonstrates better resolved-confirmation yield per provider unit **and** non-inferior terminal-positive precision against the fixed enrichment control.

### 3. Turn the governed evidence graph into a temporal, entity-resolved research frontier

**Recommendation: implement next, but only for two or three high-value action types.**

#### Limitation today

The architecture has source-bound concepts, evidence nodes and edges, playlist foundations, organic query candidates, and a portfolio allocator. In production, however, autonomous work remains dominated by text search. The graph is chiefly corroborative/offline, and the portfolio's deterministic best-first allocator operates on supplied candidates rather than generating a continuously reconciled research frontier.

That limits autonomous discovery to creators who are reachable through the current query surface. Collaborations, playlist membership, repeated external entities, community links, featured channels, and cross-language aliases can reveal creators whose own metadata never contains a known query phrase.

#### Why this is architectural

This is a reachability limitation. Better ranking of text queries cannot discover nodes that do not expose the indexed surface. A graph also becomes unsafe if identity, time, and source independence are not first-class: one syndicated description can otherwise look like several corroborating sources, and stale ownership can remain active indefinitely.

#### Proposed change

Evolve the existing graph and portfolio seam rather than create another learning store:

1. Add a canonical entity-resolution layer for creators, channels, playlists, websites, community destinations, platforms, and concepts. Store probabilistic links as assertions with exact source documents; do not merge canonical identities automatically at ambiguous thresholds.
2. Make edges temporal and observation-derived: `valid_from`, `observed_at`, freshness policy, source entity, extractor version, and retraction/supersession reason.
3. Collapse correlated observations into source families before calculating independence.
4. Materialize bounded frontier actions from approved graph patterns, initially only:
   - playlist/channel neighborhood expansion;
   - repeated external-community or website entity expansion;
   - collaboration/featured-channel expansion where the provider supplies an explicit relationship.
5. Send those actions through the same quota/review portfolio and the same unified ingestion path as search results. Record the parent edge path and marginal discoveries so rewards are attributable.
6. Publish compact, immutable graph projections for online use. Keep fuzzy resolution and the mutable graph offline, consistent with the Priority 10 boundary.
7. Apply saturation, depth, fan-out, connected-component, and source-family caps. A high-degree platform or generic website must not consume the frontier.

Avoid broad crawling as the first milestone. The point is to test whether relationship actions add unique confirmed creators per unit cost, not to maximize graph size.

#### Expected impact

- **Autonomous discovery:** adds a fundamentally different route to unknown creators.
- **Confirmation recall:** supplies independent relationship evidence for sparse channels.
- **Multilingual capability:** entity and relationship traversal can cross language surfaces without translating every query.
- **Precision:** source-family independence and temporal validity reduce false corroboration.
- **Long-term scalability:** a shared action abstraction allows future adapters without hard-wiring them into ingestion.
- **Governance/explainability:** every discovered creator retains the exact traversal path, publication pin, and evidence documents.

#### Trade-offs and cost

- Entity resolution is operationally expensive and mistakes can propagate. Prefer conservative non-merge assertions and reversible moderation over premature canonicalization.
- Provider and crawl costs vary sharply by action. Playlist expansion is likely cheaper and safer than transcript or arbitrary-web expansion.
- Graph storage will grow faster than serving catalogs. Partition immutable observations by time, compact derived projections, and retain explicit retention policies.
- Relationship expansion is vulnerable to spam rings and hub domination. Diversity caps, trust tiers, and anomaly quarantine are mandatory before promotion.
- Expected latency is mainly asynchronous and should not affect request paths; operational cost is medium-high.

#### Exit gate

Promote an action type only if a randomized capped trial shows incremental confirmed, quality-adjusted creators not reachable by the contemporaneous text-search control, at acceptable false-positive, review, and quota cost.

## Medium impact

### 4. Make each candidate investigation an idempotent, resumable workflow

**Recommendation: implement later, before materially increasing adapter count or worker concurrency.**

#### Limitation today

Search work is durable, but ingestion is still a long imperative call spanning country hydration, classification diagnostics, adaptive shadow writes, channel mutation, enrichment enqueueing, inspection, community validation, quality scoring, and learning observers. Some diagnostic/shadow writes are deliberately best-effort. Existing channel state also short-circuits later attempts.

This is reasonable at current scale, but a crash between side effects can leave a channel record, diagnostic ledger, learning contribution, and job outcome describing different attempts. Retrying safely becomes harder as the evidence controller and graph adapters add fan-out.

#### Why this is architectural

It is a consistency and execution-semantics issue, not merely better retry handling. Exactly-once external effects are unavailable; the system needs at-least-once execution with idempotent state transitions and a transactional account of what remains to be done.

#### Proposed change

- Give every investigation an immutable attempt ID and an explicit state machine.
- Commit stage outcome plus transactional outbox records in the same database transaction.
- Make every effect idempotent on `(attempt_id, stage, action_key, policy_version)`.
- Separate immutable observations from the current channel projection. Rebuild or repair projections from events rather than mutating historical attempts.
- Lease stages with heartbeats, deadlines, and deterministic stale recovery; record supersession when newer evidence makes an old attempt obsolete.
- Keep PostgreSQL and the present worker model until measured contention or isolation requirements justify another broker or service boundary.

#### Expected impact

Primarily improves operational intelligence, recovery, replay fidelity, and safe horizontal scaling. It indirectly protects classifier quality by ensuring that the decision, evidence, and learning contribution refer to the same attempt.

#### Trade-offs and cost

Migration complexity is high and immediate discovery lift is low. Event and outbox volume will increase; projections and retention need care. This should not become a broad event-sourcing rewrite: migrate only investigation stages and adapters where partial side effects are observable.

### 5. Add governed drift detection and change-triggered reactivation

**Recommendation: implement later, after unbiased evaluation cohorts exist.**

#### Limitation today

Cooldowns, UCB-style query selection, terminology decay, coverage sleeping/reactivation, and portfolio policy can react to accumulated performance. They do not yet constitute a coherent detector for distribution change. Aggregate historical reward adapts slowly when terminology, platform behavior, creator formats, model behavior, or provider coverage changes, and it can mistake a provider incident for semantic drift.

#### Why this is architectural

Autonomous adaptation needs a governed observation-to-policy path. Scattered thresholds in query, terminology, coverage, and classifier subsystems can issue conflicting responses and cannot distinguish data drift, label drift, provider drift, and policy-induced selection drift.

#### Proposed change

- Define versioned feature-distribution and outcome baselines by language/script, evidence band, provider state, discovery origin, and coverage cell.
- Run change-point and sequential tests on protected random samples, not only policy-selected traffic.
- Classify incidents into acquisition, semantic, calibration, provider, and population drift.
- Permit bounded responses only: wake a sleeping coverage cell, increase audit sampling, launch a shadow recalibration, expire a stale edge/surface, or reduce an action allocation.
- Require evaluation and approval for threshold/catalog/model changes. Drift detection proposes or triggers pre-authorized reversible policies; it never trains and deploys a model in one loop.

#### Expected impact

Improves long-term recall, multilingual coverage, operational diagnosis, and time-to-recovery while preserving governance. It is unlikely to create a large immediate lift on a stable corpus, which is why it ranks below evidence acquisition and graph reachability.

#### Trade-offs and cost

Low provider quota impact, medium analytical and storage cost, and meaningful false-alarm risk in small slices. Hierarchical pooling and minimum sample sizes are required. Without the evaluation control plane, this would automate reactions to biased traffic and should not be built.

## Low impact or unnecessary now

### End-to-end autonomous LLM researcher — do not implement

An agent that chooses arbitrary queries, browses arbitrary sources, judges its own evidence, and writes directly to the knowledge plane would collapse the acquisition, evidence, decision, and governance boundaries that currently protect precision. It would also make quota, replay, prompt injection, and causal attribution materially worse. Bounded semantic adjudication inside registered actions is the stronger design.

### A vector database or embedding service on the production decision path — not justified now

Embeddings may be useful offline for candidate generation, clustering, or audit sampling. The current limitation is not nearest-neighbor infrastructure; it is evaluation coverage and evidence selection. Adding a mutable similarity index to online classification would weaken deterministic replay unless a demonstrated scale/latency requirement and immutable snapshot protocol exist.

### Microservice decomposition or a new message broker — unnecessary now

The modular monolith plus PostgreSQL job model is an advantage while contracts are evolving. Split services only when measured database contention, independent scaling, security isolation, or deployment ownership requires it. First add attempt IDs, outbox semantics, and idempotent stages; moving unreliable boundaries across the network would not improve them.

### More static vocabulary, country rules, or hard-coded quality terms — do not implement as architecture

Curated terms remain valid controls and emergency fallback data, but extending them is maintenance rather than architectural evolution. In particular, the separate legacy quality scorer still uses fixed technical and hype phrases; it should eventually consume governed classifier/knowledge-plane outputs or remain a non-authoritative compatibility score rather than gain more hand-authored terms.

### Immediate retirement of legacy stores and curated fallbacks — do not do yet

Priority 10 correctly provides a common publication boundary, but the authoritative and governed paths are still being compared and canaried. Deleting compatibility stores before representative replay, consumer pinning, and rollback proof would reduce resilience without improving discovery. Retirement is cleanup after evidence, not a strategic initiative.

## Recommended sequence

| Order | Investment | Timing | Principal gate |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evaluation and calibration control plane | Now | Unbiased time-split estimates with confidence bounds and review provenance |
| 2 | Value-of-information evidence controller | Now, following cohort contract | Better resolved-confirmation yield per provider unit with non-inferior precision |
| 3 | Temporal entity-resolved research frontier | Next, adapter by adapter | Incremental confirmed creators versus contemporaneous text-search control |
| 4 | Resumable investigation workflow | Before adapter/concurrency scale-up | Crash/retry drills produce one coherent attempt and no duplicate effects |
| 5 | Drift detection and bounded reactivation | Later | Low false-alarm rate on protected samples and reversible responses |

## Final assessment

The engine does **not** need another broad roadmap of capabilities. Its conceptual architecture is already stronger than a conventional keyword crawler or monolithic AI classifier. The next evolution should make the existing loop scientifically measurable, selective about evidence cost, and capable of traversing governed relationships beyond text search.

If only one investment is approved, choose the evaluation and calibration control plane. If two are approved, add the value-of-information evidence controller. Those two create the trustworthy feedback substrate on which model promotion, multilingual expansion, graph exploration, drift response, and eventual legacy retirement can be based. Without that substrate, further autonomous features would increase activity faster than knowledge.
